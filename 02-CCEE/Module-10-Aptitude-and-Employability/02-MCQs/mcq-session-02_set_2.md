## 📖 Full Explanations

---

### Q01 — First Term of a Ratio
**Correct Answer: C) Antecedent**

- **Why C is correct:** In a ratio a:b, the first term 'a' is formally called the **antecedent** and the second term 'b' is the **consequent**. These are the standard terms in ratio terminology.
- **Why A is wrong:** Consequent is the second term 'b' — not the first.
- **Why B is wrong:** Denominator is a fraction term. In ratio language, 'b' (second term) is the consequent — not the denominator.
- **Why D is wrong:** Mean refers to the middle two terms (b and c) in a proportion a:b::c:d — not the first term of a standalone ratio.

---

### Q02 — Triplicate Ratio of 2:3
**Correct Answer: C) 8:27**

- **Why C is correct:** Triplicate ratio = cube each term. 2³:3³ = 8:27.
- **Why A is wrong:** 4:9 = 2²:3² — this is the **duplicate** ratio (squaring), not triplicate.
- **Why B is wrong:** 6:9 = 3×(2:3) — just scaling the ratio by multiplying both terms by 3, not cubing.
- **Why D is wrong:** 16:81 = 2⁴:3⁴ — fourth power; this has no standard name and is not the triplicate ratio.

---

### Q03 — Finding A:C from A:B and B:C
**Correct Answer: A) 5:9**

- **Why A is correct:** A:B = 5:7 and B:C = 7:9. The B term is already equal (7) in both ratios. So A:C = 5:9 directly. Alternatively: A/C = (A/B) × (B/C) = (5/7) × (7/9) = 5/9. ✅
- **Why B is wrong:** 7:9 = B:C — one of the given ratios; gives the relationship between B and C, not A and C.
- **Why C is wrong:** 5:7 = A:B — the other given ratio; gives the relationship between A and B, not A and C.
- **Why D is wrong:** 35:63 = 5×7 : 7×9 — this is the unsimplified product before cancelling the common factor 7. Always reduce: 35:63 = 5:9.

---

### Q04 — Comparing 2:3 and 3:4
**Correct Answer: B) 3:4**

- **Why B is correct:** Cross multiply to compare a:b vs c:d — compare a×d with b×c. Here: 2×4 = 8 and 3×3 = 9. Since 8 < 9, we have 2:3 < 3:4. So **3:4 is the greater ratio**. (In decimal: 2/3 ≈ 0.667 and 3/4 = 0.75.)
- **Why A is wrong:** 2:3 = 0.667 which is less than 3:4 = 0.75 — 2:3 is the smaller ratio, not the greater.
- **Why C is wrong:** 2/3 ≠ 3/4. They are clearly unequal — cross product confirms: 8 ≠ 9.
- **Why D is wrong:** Both ratios are fully defined numbers. Comparison is always possible using cross multiplication.

---

### Q05 — Dividing ₹7,200 in Ratio 3:4:5
**Correct Answer: C) ₹3,000**

- **Why C is correct:** Total parts = 3+4+5 = 12. C's share (5 parts) = 7,200 × 5/12 = 600 × 5 = **₹3,000**.
- **Why A is wrong:** ₹1,800 = 7,200 × 3/12 — this is A's share (3 parts), not C's (5 parts).
- **Why B is wrong:** ₹2,400 = 7,200 × 4/12 — this is B's share (4 parts), not C's.
- **Why D is wrong:** ₹3,600 = 7,200/2 — equal halving ignores the ratio; also exceeds C's actual entitlement.

---

### Q06 — Inverse Ratio of 5:8
**Correct Answer: B) 8:5**

- **Why B is correct:** Inverse ratio = swap both terms. Inverse of a:b = b:a. So inverse of 5:8 = **8:5**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 5:8 is the original ratio — not inverted.
- **Why C is wrong:** 25:64 = 5²:8² — this is the **duplicate** ratio, not the inverse.
- **Why D is wrong:** 1:8 arbitrarily replaces only the first term with 1 — no rule for inverse ratio works this way.

---

### Q07 — Compound Ratio of 3:4, 8:9, and 2:3
**Correct Answer: C) 4:9**

- **Why C is correct:** Compound ratio = multiply all numerators : multiply all denominators.
  - Numerators: 3 × 8 × 2 = 48
  - Denominators: 4 × 9 × 3 = 108
  - GCD(48, 108) = 12
  - Simplified: 48÷12 : 108÷12 = **4:9** ✅
- **Why A is wrong:** 1:2 = 48:96 — the denominator product is 108, not 96; arithmetic error in denominator.
- **Why B is wrong:** 2:3 = 48:72 — again wrong denominator product; 4×9×3 = 108, not 72.
- **Why D is wrong:** 3:8 picks the first numerator and last denominator of the three ratios — no multiplication performed at all.

---

### Q08 — Two Numbers in Ratio 3:5, Sum = 160
**Correct Answer: D) 100**

- **Why D is correct:** Let numbers = 3k and 5k. Sum: 8k = 160 → k = 20. Larger number = 5k = 5×20 = **100**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 60 = 3×20 — this is the **smaller** number (3 parts), not the larger (5 parts).
- **Why B is wrong:** 80 = 160/2 — equal halving; completely ignores the 3:5 ratio.
- **Why C is wrong:** 96 = 160 × 3/5 — applies the wrong fraction; large number = 5/(3+5) of total = 5/8 of 160, not 3/5.

---

### Q09 — Finding x in 4:x = x:16
**Correct Answer: C) 8**

- **Why C is correct:** x is the mean proportional between 4 and 16. Using the formula: x² = 4×16 = 64 → x = √64 = **8**. Verify: 4:8 = 8:16 = 1:2 ✅ — both ratios are equal.
- **Why A is wrong:** x=4 gives 4:4 = 4:16 → 1:1 ≠ 1:4. Ratios are not equal.
- **Why B is wrong:** x=6 gives 4:6 = 6:16 → 2:3 ≠ 3:8. Cross-check: 4×16=64 but 6×6=36 ≠ 64.
- **Why D is wrong:** x=10 gives 4:10 = 10:16 → 2:5 ≠ 5:8. Cross-check: 4×16=64 but 10×10=100 ≠ 64.

---

### Q10 — Means in Proportion 3:6::5:10
**Correct Answer: B) 6 and 5**

- **Why B is correct:** In a:b::c:d, the **means** are the inner two terms b and c. In 3:6::5:10, b=6 (second term of first ratio) and c=5 (first term of second ratio). Means = **6 and 5**. Verify: extremes×means → 3×10=30 and 6×5=30 ✅.
- **Why A is wrong:** 3 and 10 are the **extremes** (outermost terms), not the means.
- **Why C is wrong:** 3 and 5 are both antecedents (first terms of each ratio pair) — not the standard definition of means in proportion.
- **Why D is wrong:** 6 and 10 are both consequents (second terms of each ratio) — not the means; means must be the two middle terms spanning both ratios.

---

### Q11 — Invertendo Property
**Correct Answer: C) b/a = d/c**

- **Why C is correct:** Invertendo means inverting both sides of the equality simultaneously. If a/b = c/d, flipping both gives b/a = d/c. Both sides are inverted together.
- **Why A is wrong:** a/c = b/d is **Alternendo** — the means (b and c) are swapped; positions are exchanged, not inverted.
- **Why B is wrong:** (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d is **Componendo** — the denominator is added to the numerator on both sides.
- **Why D is wrong:** (a−b)/b = (c−d)/d is **Dividendo** — the denominator is subtracted from the numerator on both sides.

---

### Q12 — Find (2a+3b):(3a+2b) given a:b = 3:5
**Correct Answer: A) 21:19**

- **Why A is correct:** Let a = 3k, b = 5k.
  - 2a + 3b = 2(3k) + 3(5k) = 6k + 15k = 21k
  - 3a + 2b = 3(3k) + 2(5k) = 9k + 10k = 19k
  - Ratio = 21k : 19k = **21:19** ✅
- **Why B is wrong:** 9:15 = 3a:3b — picks only the 'a' terms with coefficient 3; ignores the 'b' cross terms entirely.
- **Why C is wrong:** 19:21 — correct numbers but reversed; swaps numerator and denominator of the right answer.
- **Why D is wrong:** 6:10 = 2a:2b — applies coefficient 2 to both variables, ignoring that the expressions have mixed terms (2a+3b ≠ 2a).

---

### Q13 — Mean Proportional Between 4 and 36
**Correct Answer: C) 12**

- **Why C is correct:** Mean proportional = √(a×b) = √(4×36) = √144 = **12**. Verify: 4:12 = 12:36 = 1:3 ✅.
- **Why A is wrong:** 20 = (4+36)/2 — this is the arithmetic mean, not the geometric mean (mean proportional requires √(ab), not (a+b)/2).
- **Why B is wrong:** 40 = 4+36 — plain addition; no formula for mean proportional uses simple addition.
- **Why D is wrong:** 18 = 36/2 — halves the larger number arbitrarily; √(4×36) = √144 = 12, not 18.

---

### Q14 — Name of (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d
**Correct Answer: D) Componendo**

- **Why D is correct:** Componendo means "adding" — specifically, adding the denominator to the numerator on both sides of the proportion. If a/b = c/d → (a+b)/b = (c+d)/d. The name comes from "component" — a part being added.
- **Why A is wrong:** Invertendo flips both fractions: b/a = d/c — nothing is added.
- **Why B is wrong:** Alternendo swaps the means: a/c = b/d — positions of b and c are exchanged.
- **Why C is wrong:** Dividendo subtracts the denominator from the numerator: (a−b)/b = (c−d)/d — subtraction, not addition.

---

### Q15 — Find x:y:z given x:y = 2:3 and y:z = 4:5
**Correct Answer: B) 8:12:15**

- **Why B is correct:** Make y common. y = 3 in x:y and y = 4 in y:z. LCM(3,4) = 12.
  - Multiply x:y = 2:3 by 4 → x:y = 8:12
  - Multiply y:z = 4:5 by 3 → y:z = 12:15
  - Combined: x:y:z = **8:12:15** ✅
- **Why A is wrong:** 2:3:5 takes x and y from first ratio and z from second without making y common — y is 3 in the first and 4 in the second, which are different values.
- **Why C is wrong:** 2:4:5 replaces y with 4 (from y:z) losing the correct scaling of x relative to the unified y.
- **Why D is wrong:** 6:12:15 multiplies x:y by 3 instead of 4. Since LCM(3,4)=12, the first ratio must be multiplied by 4 (not 3), giving x=8 not x=6.

### Q16 — Largest of Three Numbers in Ratio 1:2:3, Sum = 180

- **Why C is correct:** Total parts = 1+2+3 = 6. Largest number (3 parts) = 180 × 3/6 = 180 × 1/2 = **90**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 30 = 180 × 1/6 — this is the smallest number (1 part), not the largest.
- **Why B is wrong:** 60 = 180 × 2/6 — this is the middle number (2 parts), not the largest.
- **Why D is wrong:** 120 = 180 × 4/6 — uses 4 parts, but the largest share in a 1:2:3 ratio is only 3 parts.

### Q17 — y Varies as x², Find y when x = 5
**Correct Answer: C) 50**

- **Why C is correct:** y ∝ x² → y = kx². When y=18, x=3: k = 18/3² = 18/9 = 2. When x=5: y = 2×5² = 2×25 = **50**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 30 = 2×3×5 — treats the relationship as y=k×x×(original x) rather than y=kx².
- **Why B is wrong:** 40 = 2×20 — computes 5² as 20 instead of 25; arithmetic error in squaring.
- **Why D is wrong:** 60 = 2×30 — uses 5²=30, another squaring error; 5² is 25, not 30.

---

### Q18 — x Varies Inversely as y², Find x when y = 6
**Correct Answer: C) 1**

- **Why C is correct:** x ∝ 1/y² → x·y² = k. When x=4, y=3: k = 4×3² = 4×9 = 36. When y=6: x = 36/6² = 36/36 = **1**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 2 = 36/18 — computes y=6 as 6¹ (not squared) in the denominator; treats as simple inverse (xy=k) instead of xy²=k.
- **Why B is wrong:** 4 = original x — fails to substitute the new y=6; no calculation performed.
- **Why D is wrong:** 8 = 4×2 — doubles the original x with no mathematical basis; ignores the new y entirely.

---

### Q19 — Graph of Inverse Variation
**Correct Answer: D) A hyperbola**

- **Why D is correct:** Inverse variation gives xy = k → y = k/x. This is a rectangular **hyperbola** — as x increases y decreases, and the curve never touches either axis.
- **Why A is wrong:** Straight line through origin = graph of **direct** variation (y = kx). The origin intercept is zero.
- **Why B is wrong:** Straight line NOT through origin = graph of **partial variation** (y = kx + c, c≠0) — a linear but shifted relationship.
- **Why C is wrong:** Parabola = graph of **quadratic** variation (y = kx²) — a different type of non-linear relationship.

---

### Q20 — Inverse Proportion: Men Needed to Finish in 6 Days
**Correct Answer: C) 20**

- **Why C is correct:** More men → fewer days → inverse proportion. 15×8 = n×6 → n = 120/6 = **20 men**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 15 = original workforce — unchanged; ignores that fewer days require more workers.
- **Why B is wrong:** 18 = 15 + (8−6+1) — arbitrary arithmetic; the correct approach is cross-multiplication.
- **Why D is wrong:** 24 = 15×8/5 — divides by 5 instead of 6; off by one in the denominator.

### Q21 — Partial Variation: Find x when y = 6
**Correct Answer: B) 18**

- **Why B is correct:** Partial variation: x = ky + c.
  - Condition 1 (y=2, x=10): 2k + c = 10 ...(i)
  - Condition 2 (y=4, x=14): 4k + c = 14 ...(ii)
  - Subtract (i) from (ii): 2k = 4 → k = 2
  - Substitute back: c = 10 − 2(2) = 6
  - When y=6: x = 2(6) + 6 = 12 + 6 = **18** ✅
- **Why A is wrong:** 16 = 2(6)+4 — uses c=4 instead of the correct c=6; error in solving simultaneous equations.
- **Why C is wrong:** 20 = 2(6)+8 — uses c=8; another incorrect value from wrong arithmetic.
- **Why D is wrong:** 22 = 2(8)+6 — substitutes y=8 instead of the given y=6; reads the question incorrectly.

---

### Q22 — Joint Variation: Find z when x=2, y=7
**Correct Answer: B) 28**

- **Why B is correct:** z = kxy. When z=30, x=5, y=3: k = 30/(5×3) = 30/15 = 2. When x=2, y=7: z = 2×2×7 = **28**.
- **Why A is wrong:** 25 = 2×(2+7+4) — adds instead of multiplies x and y; joint variation requires multiplication.
- **Why C is wrong:** 30 = original z — uses original values without substituting new x=2 and y=7.
- **Why D is wrong:** 35 = 2×5×7 — uses the original x=5 instead of the new x=2; substitution error.

---

### Q26 — Partner Withdraws Capital Mid-Year
**Correct Answer: B) 6:7**

- **Why B is correct:**
  - A's equivalent = 60,000 × 12 = 7,20,000
  - B's equivalent = (80,000×6) + (60,000×6) = 4,80,000 + 3,60,000 = 8,40,000
  - Ratio = 7,20,000 : 8,40,000 → divide both by 1,20,000 → **6:7** ✅
- **Why A is wrong:** 3:4 = ratio of A's capital to B's initial capital alone — neither time nor withdrawal is factored in.
- **Why C is wrong:** 4:5 results from using only B's final capital (60,000×12) without accounting for the first 6 months at ₹80,000.
- **Why D is wrong:** 7:6 = reversing the correct ratio — B's weighted investment is larger, so B's share is the larger portion, not A's.

### Q30 — Identifying Sleeping Partner
**Correct Answer: B) A partner who only invests capital but does not participate in management**

- **Why B is correct:** A **sleeping (dormant) partner** contributes capital to the business but takes no active role in its day-to-day operations or management. Their share is purely proportional to their investment.
- **Why A is wrong:** A partner who manages the business AND invests capital is an **active (working) partner** — the exact opposite of a sleeping partner.
- **Why C is wrong:** A partner with unlimited liability for losses describes a **general partner** in legal terminology — a separate classification unrelated to active vs. sleeping distinction.
- **Why D is wrong:** A partner who shares only in profits but not losses describes a specific legal arrangement that is not the standard definition of a sleeping partner. By default, both profit and loss are shared in the same ratio.
